# Discovery, marketplace and business model

## Launch strategy

A useful independent wedding workspace first. Couples can add any vendor/venue name, link, proposal or contact manually; personal collection searchable/filterable. Optional licensed local search adapter brings results into personal shortlists only within provider rights. R4 verified profiles, inquiry/booking workflows, reviews and commerce after supply and moderation strategy validated. No scraping protected websites or fabricated catalogs. Vendor list empty state remains useful. Core app remains independent of marketplace.

## Search and profile requirements

Filter location/radius, category, venue type/style, capacity (ceremony/seated/standing), indoor/outdoor, price basis/estimate, dates, catering/bar policy, accessibility, accommodation/parking, rain backup, included rentals, curfew/restrictions. Use unknown filter handling explicitly; do not exclude all unknown attributes silently. Profile includes source URL and checked date, business contacts, portfolio/media rights, package scope, provenance of price, availability, policies and permits where provided. Trust label: user-entered, licensed listing, vendor-confirmed, unknown; avoid blanket "verified" for unverified fields. Reviews require genuine moderation and source rights; no fake stars. Availability confirmed only by authorized provider/vendor response, not opening hours. Save personal records with source snapshots where permitted; comply with provider cache, attribution, retention and display restrictions.

## Ranking and booking boundaries

Preference match based on user criteria and coverage, not paid placements. Clearly distinguish sponsored from organic. Inquiries require user approval and sender status; no implied reservation or binding booking from a shortlist. R1 "booked" is user recording a real agreement. R4 direct booking/payment only with verified contracts, cancellation terms, provider confirmations, refund workflows and legal review. No raw card storage. Hotel/travel information separate from confirmed booking. Allow opt-out of vendor invitations and marketing.

## Pricing hypotheses (not approved)

| Plan | Example price to test | Proposed value |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Core tasks, guests, budget, basic seating, site, manual discovery |
| Wedding Plus | $14.99/month per wedding | AI extraction, comparisons, advanced seating/packets |
| Complete Wedding | $149 one-time | Extended access through wedding, premium templates and operations/archive |
| Professional | from $49/month | Multi-wedding, reusable templates, branded packets and client portal |

No billing until owner approves entitlements, term/duration, storage/AI quotas, taxes, cancellation/refund terms, legal and accessibility obligations, and costs. Do not paywall access to essential data exports or make misleading free claims. Test willingness to pay, provider costs and retention; optional vendor subscriptions/disclosed fees later. Define transparent entitlement checks server-side; pricing page may explain future plans only if clearly marked, no active purchase button until configured.

## Validation plan

Interview couples at various stages, professional planners and vendors. Benchmark tasks: find comparable venue, normalize proposal, collect RSVP, reassign seat, check budget, generate handoff; measure completion rate/time, mobile friction and errors. Start with manually curated authorized pilot data, not a massive marketplace build. Analytics event names + counts only, no guest identity, contract text or message data. Review support burden, cost per wedding and data rights before expanding.
