## Proposed competitive advantage

- Connected data and deterministic dependency handling: guest changes inform seating, meals, forecast and packet freshness.
- Exceptional apples-to-apples comparison with evidence, unknowns, preference weighting and decision snapshots.
- Practical AI with approval and deterministic rules, not autonomous unsupported answers.
- High-quality scoped wedding packages and coordinator handoff.
- Calm, elegant mobile-first experience, accessibility and manual fallbacks.

These are differentiation hypotheses, not claims of market exclusivity. Direct competitors include AI-assisted and operational wedding systems; indirect competitors include marketplaces, registries, websites and task planners. Validate through customer interviews and real product trials; evaluate time to accomplish the same end-to-end tasks, error rates, source accuracy, mobile usability and collaboration. Do not use unverified market share, pricing or acquisition claims in launch copy.
